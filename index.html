    Questions

    Good to know.

    Where does the music come from?

    From YouTube. MoodBook searches for long, embeddable mixes that fit each scene and plays them in a built-in player. Nothing is downloaded or re-hosted.

    Does it work for books in other languages?

    Yes. Type the title as you know it, in Ukrainian, Spanish, Korean or anything else. MoodBook identifies the book and composes for it.

    What if it doesn't know my book?

    It still composes from the title and genre it can infer, and tells you so. Picking the book from the suggestion list gives the best result.

    Can I listen on my phone?

    Yes. MoodBook is a website that works on any phone or tablet. Add it to your home screen for an app-like experience.

    Is my library private?

    Your shelf and liked tracks are stored in your browser only. Account sync arrives with Pro.

    How much does it cost?

    Three books a day are free. Pro is $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year and opens unlimited books and sync.
