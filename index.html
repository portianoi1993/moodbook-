    02

    How it works

    1. 01

      Name the book

      Any title, any language. Start typing and pick it from the list, or just press Compose.

    2. 02

      We read the room

      Genre, setting, tone and pace are turned into six scenes, from the quiet chapters to the ones you can't put down.

    3. 03

      Press play

      Six long instrumental mixes, sourced from YouTube, play in a small dock that stays with you while you read.

    03

    Why instrumental

    Lyrics compete with the sentence you're reading. A soundtrack shouldn't. Every mix MoodBook picks is lyric-free and long enough to carry a whole chapter.

    • Matched, not generic. A desert epic gets wind and drums. A self-help book gets steady focus beats.
    • Scenes, not playlists. Switch from "Quiet Night Reading" to "Battle Surge" when the chapter turns.
    • Yours to keep. Save books to a shelf and heart the mixes you want again.

    05

    Questions

    Where does the music come from?

    From YouTube. MoodBook searches for long, embeddable instrumental mixes that fit each scene and plays them in a built-in player. Nothing is downloaded or re-hosted.

    Does it work for books in other languages?

    Yes. Type the title as you know it, in Ukrainian, Spanish, Korean or anything else. MoodBook identifies the book and composes for it.

    What if it doesn't know my book?

    It still composes from the title and genre it can infer, and tells you so. Picking the book from the suggestion list gives the best result.

    Can I listen on my phone?

    Yes. MoodBook is a website that works on any phone or tablet. Add it to your home screen for an app-like experience.

    Is my library private?

    Your shelf and liked tracks are stored in your browser only. Account sync arrives with Pro.

    How much does it cost?

    Three books a day are free. Pro is $9.99 a month or $99.99 a year and opens unlimited books and sync.
